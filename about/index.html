About

School of Computing and Mathematical Sciences

The roots of mathematics and statistics at Birkbeck, are deeply intertwined with the institution's founding mission of providing education to working people. While the School of Computing and Mathematical Sciences was formally established more recently, bringing together computing, mathematics, and statistics, the teaching and research in these fields have a long and distinguished history at the college.

Birkbeck's commitment to widening access to education dates back to its foundation as the London Mechanics' Institute in 1823. This ethos has been central to the provision of mathematics and statistics degrees, offering flexible, part-time evening study that has enabled generations of students to pursue higher education while balancing work and family life. The department has been home to prominent figures in the history of mathematics, including Louis Mordell, who conducted research at Birkbeck. More recently, the department has faced challenges but has been supported by mathematical societies across the UK, who have recognized Birkbeck's unique and crucial role in making mathematical sciences accessible to mature students. Today, the department continues this tradition, offering a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs and contributing to a rich research environment in areas such as mathematical finance, mathematical physics, and applied statistics.
