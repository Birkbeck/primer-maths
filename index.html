School of Computing and Mathematical Sciences

This intensive short course is designed to prepare students for the mathematical and logical demands of both undergraduate and postgraduate degree programmes in the mathematical sciences at Birkbeck, University of London. The course will:

  • Consolidate and extend key topics from A-level Mathematics, providing a strong foundation for undergraduate study.

  • Provide a rigorous refresher of core mathematical concepts for students entering postgraduate programmes.

  • Introduce the abstract and formal approach to mathematics characteristic of university-level study.

  • Develop advanced problem-solving skills and mathematical communication.

  • Build confidence in tackling unseen mathematical problems at an appropriate level.
