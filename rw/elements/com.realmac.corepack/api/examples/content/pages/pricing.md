## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Yes! We offer a 10% discount for annual payments on all plans.

### What's included in the setup fee?

Setup fees cover initial design, development, and deployment of your website or application.

### Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! All plans include support, with priority support available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. We'll help you export your data and ensure a smooth transition.
